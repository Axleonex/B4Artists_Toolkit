Watch each case at least twice and inspect front, side and oblique views. Judge motion naturalness, planted-contact stability, transition continuity and preservation of the stated task intent. Scores run from 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (excellent).

Both sides play at their own source frame rate. The shorter motion holds at its end; the loop boundary is not part of the animation. Correction estimates mean fixes needed; interactions mean editor actions needed. Leave estimates blank if unsure.

## Current official product basis

The protocol was researched on 2026-09-10 from Cascadeur's official documentation:

- [AutoPosing](https://cascadeur.com/help/tools/animation_tools/autoposing) describes controller-driven whole-body posing on a standard Cascadeur rig. It documents humanoid and quadruped support, with quadruped availability restricted by edition.
- [Inbetweening](https://cascadeur.com/help/category/278) describes local AI interpolation from selected priority keys, including partial-track operation and optional foot cleaning. The documented limits include humanoids, a standard rig, bounded key spacing, limited motion coverage, a ground plane rather than general scene understanding, and physics as a separate operation.
- [AutoPhysics](https://cascadeur.com/help/tools/physics_tools/autophysics) describes a physics-derived assistant result, priority frames, control preservation and Snap to Physics.
- [Fulcrum points](https://cascadeur.com/help/tools/physics_tools/fulcrum_points) documents support/contact analysis and motion cleaning.
- [Animation pipeline](https://cascadeur.com/help/animation_pipeline) supplies the intended staging around posing, interpolation and physical refinement.

The installed Cascadeur version, edition, entitlement and exact settings must be recorded at execution time. Documentation availability does not prove that a feature is enabled by a free entitlement. The standalone B4Artists workflow remains the product requirement; the optional connector is outside this protocol.

## Matched work

The eight task families are reach, crouch, walk, run, jump, land, turn and difficult transition. Each pair uses the same character mesh and skeleton, 30 fps, frames 1-25, authored priority poses and priority times, world-space task basis, ground and intended contacts. Conversion time is measured separately. Input priority matrices must agree within `1e-5`.

Three portable FBX characters are required: standard proportions, tall/long-limbed proportions and short/broad proportions. Their hashes, unit scales and rest poses must be frozen before execution.

The workflow mappings are:

1. B4ML whole-body controls and pins versus equivalent Cascadeur AutoPosing controllers and fixed controls.
2. B4ML whole-body motion from the same priorities versus Cascadeur Inbetweening with the same interval, timing, track scope and recorded style setting. A B4ML learned comparison cannot run until a learned provider passes its independent qualification gates.
3. B4ML explicit contact intervals and geometric correction versus recorded Cascadeur foot-cleaning and fulcrum settings.
4. B4ML COM flight/contact refinement versus Cascadeur AutoPhysics using equivalent priorities and preservation intent.
5. Editable B4ML candidate plus source restoration versus saved Cascadeur output and review FBX, with any unbaking or fixed-interpolation step recorded.

One cold and three warm runs are retained for each case. Failed runs remain in the record. Raw event logs, hardware and background load are required.

## Measurements and claims

Automated measures cover priority and pin error, contact drift, penetration, limb stretch, rotation-velocity discontinuity, center-of-mass flight error, cold/warm elapsed time, longest interactive pause and peak working set. Blind human measures cover naturalness, contact stability, transition continuity, intent preservation, production acceptability, actual corrective edits, actual interactions, active work time, overall preference and visible failure tags.

At least three independent animators must complete all 24 matched cases; at least one must have five or more years of animation experience. Labels remain hidden, A/B order is balanced, playback presentation is identical, ratings lock before identity reveal and hands-on sessions are counterbalanced.

Parity requires all 24 cases, all hashes, no median human-dimension deficit greater than 0.25, no weakened automated safety threshold and no hidden failed/excluded case. A superiority claim additionally requires median gains of at least 0.25 in two named human-quality dimensions and at least 20% lower values in two efficiency measures, with each claim supported by at least six task families. Remaining regressions must be reported.

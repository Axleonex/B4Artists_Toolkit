# Data options checked during v17

On September8,2026, the [official Ubisoft LAFAN1 repository](https://github.com/ubisoft/ubisoft-laforge-animation-dataset) documents77 sequences, five subjects and roughly4.6 hours at30fps. It states a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives4.0 license. Its broader motion collection is relevant research context, but distribution suitability for this add-on is unresolved; no code, dataset, weights or archive was acquired. Dataset size alone does not resolve entitlement or applicability.

The [author's Learned Motion Matching overview](https://theorangeduck.com/page/learned-motion-matching) describes three specialized networks replacing motion-matching components, reducing the need to retain source animation in memory. This is a possible architectural research direction; its locomotion-controller results do not establish arbitrary keyframe inbetweening or Cascadeur parity for our tool. No implementation was copied.

A fresh [CMU publisher retrieval](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/) again timed out. Existing pinned conversion provenance is retained; current publisher re-verification remains unresolved. No new download, split ownership change or licensing acceptance occurred.

Decision: complete the frozen training-only readout diagnosis first. A subsequent data expansion must be bounded, prospectively specified and rights-checked, preserve every existing holdout, and add distinct motion coverage rather than inflating the count of overlapping windows. Broad learned animation, styles, partial-body controls and true comparative/human evaluation remain required.


Update: direct HTTP publisher/FAQ retrieval succeeded after the web retriever timed out. See DISTRIBUTION-RECHECK-v17.md and its checksum receipts. The fresh retrieval gap is resolved for the existing CMU source; no other dataset or asset is thereby qualified.

# Explicit whole-body controls: experimental 0.6.0

The overall goal remains ACTIVE and incomplete. This milestone adds geometric rotation and pole constraints to the existing local learned whole-body workflow. It does not add newly trained weights, learned orientation conditioning, anatomical limits, physics or learned animation.

## Animator workflow

Start a new whole-body preview. The six position targets now show axes. Enable Rotation beside a target to use its world orientation for the pelvis, head, hand or foot; rotate the helper in Object Mode. Without that checkbox, the original authored orientation remains the goal. Position and rotation are independently selectable, except the pelvis position stays pinned.

Each hand and foot has an additional pole helper. Enable Elbow Direction or Knee Direction and move the corresponding helper to choose the limb's bend plane and side. A pole is a direction hint around the limb axis, not a requested elbow/knee position and not an anatomical joint-limit setting. Near-straight or incompatible configurations may fail and restore the preceding preview; adjust the target or disable that pole.

Target Strength blends helper positions and rotations from the captured starting state. Pole helper displacement is blended from its initial location; at zero strength pole constraints are omitted. Learned Influence independently controls the unchanged neural suggestion, so Target Strength zero alone is not a general guarantee of restoring every unconstrained joint when learned influence is nonzero.

Solve, inspect, keep as an editable FK anchor, or cancel. Rotation/pole changes after solving require a new solve before keeping. Changes while a solve runs invalidate its result and restore the preceding verified preview. Save/reload retains enabled controls and helper transforms; source actions and rig modes remain recoverable.

Previews saved by 0.5.1 remain keepable. Finish or cancel an older preview, then start a new one to obtain initialized rotation axes and pole helpers. The old position-only signature is preserved when extra controls are inactive.

## Solver contract

The Session API accepts orientations_world keyed by semantic indices 0, 4, 7, 10, 13 and 16, with unit quaternions in w/x/y/z order. pole_targets uses elbow/knee indices 6, 9, 12 and 15 with world-space points. Both dictionaries are optional. Nonfinite/invalid vectors and unknown joints are rejected before applying a solve.

Pelvis orientation participates in whole-body fitting. End-effector orientations are applied through the writable controls and verified on the original rig. Pole residuals compare normalized perpendicular bend directions; collapsing a limb to straight cannot satisfy that objective. An initial geometric limb solve chooses the requested bend side before iterative fitting. The initializer may shorten its provisional reach by 0.5% to avoid a singular start; final acceptance still uses the original requested pins and unchanged tolerances, not the provisional targets.

Acceptance requires pin error below 2e-4 reference torso lengths, tracked bone-length change below 0.002, orientation error below 0.001 radians, and enabled pole direction error below 0.01 radians. These numerical gates are not visual or anatomical quality evidence. Requests with extra controls currently use up to 80 iterations; the original position-only workflow retains its 50-iteration budget.

The original rig remains the final authority even when the private evaluation proxy is used. Only accepted iterates are displayed at cooperative checkpoints. Source preservation, proxy cleanup and cancellation remain part of the same workflow.

## Development evidence

The first raw bend-vector residual allowed the optimizer to satisfy a pole by straightening the limb. Fresh derivatives did not fix the BoneForge failure. A geometric initialization and normalized directional objective resolved the measured case. An intermediate initialization also exposed an incorrect reliance on adapter iteration order; it was corrected to explicit arm/leg IDs. Failed v1-v3 runs and their logs are retained; v4-v6 record the corrected implementation and lifecycle tests.

The dedicated tests use feasible targets authored on five actual rigs with object rotation, translation and uniform scale: BoneForge controls, Rigify basic/default generated humans, and basic/default metarigs. They check requested positions, control and evaluated-joint orientations, pole directions and bone lengths. Further cases cover selective rotation without a position pin, zero strength, invalid inputs, changed targets, mid-solve rollback, save/reload, and an actual preview generated by the retained 0.5.1 ZIP. None of these fixtures constitutes production-mesh or animator-quality acceptance.

## Remaining requirements

Anatomical/twist limits, collision and contact intervals, balance/COM, gravity/momentum, learned temporal motion, secondary motion, quadrupeds, imported rig behavior, production assets, the optional connector and direct Cascadeur/animator comparisons remain open. The contextual model's prior-pose regressions remain documented. Continuous live posing and startup latency also remain unfinished.

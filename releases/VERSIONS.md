## Identify a build

Read the add-on version in Preferences > Add-ons or in the ZIP's top-level `__init__.py` `bl_info` field. Ghost Tool 4.3.1 is the current source, installation archive and manual version. Its [package receipt](../docs/ghost_tool/releases/4.3.1/package-receipt.json) records ZIP integrity, the 4.3.1 version and byte-for-byte source matching. Ghost Tool 4.2.11 remains available as a historical standalone download. Its [package receipt](../docs/ghost_tool/releases/4.2.11/package-receipt.json) records 123 passing startup/toolbar checks and the separate width-suite limitation. The earlier 4.2.9 release retains its 89-check receipt as history. The earlier Ghost Tool 4.2.8 [package receipt](../docs/ghost_tool/releases/4.2.8/package-receipt.json) records the full checksum, source matching and 77 passing focused tests/checks in native Bforartists. The earlier 4.2.7 package and its 148-check receipt are historical. The earlier 4.2.6 archive and its 178-check stand-in receipt are historical. The 4.2.5 archive and reports are historical. The concurrently merged 4.2.4 interface cleanup is retained. The earlier 4.2.2 [package receipt](../docs/ghost_tool/authoring-pause/package-receipt.json) records that build's evidence. The published 4.2.0 prerelease used internal revision r4. Older experimental archives used build counters in filenames; check the internal version and checksum to identify a build.
